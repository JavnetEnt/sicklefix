Schools Mode

A class quiz for one projector. Pupils vote by raising hands, and the teacher taps in the counts. No names or pupil data are collected.

Tips for teachers

  • Ask pupils to raise a hand for Myth or Fact, then count each group and tap the + buttons.
  • Read each explanation aloud and ask, "Have you heard this before?"
  • A pupil in your class may have sickle cell, or have a family member who does. Never single anyone out. Let them speak only if they choose to.
  • Press the full-screen key (F11) on a projector for the best view.
